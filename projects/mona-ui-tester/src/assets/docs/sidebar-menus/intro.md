# Sidebar Menus

**Selectors:** `[monaSidebarGroup]`, `ul[monaSidebarMenu]`, `li[monaSidebarMenuItem]`, `a[monaSidebarMenuButton]`, `button[monaSidebarMenuButton]`, `mona-sidebar-menu-skeleton`, and the parts below

What goes inside a [sidebar](/components/sidebar): titled groups, lists of rows, and what a row can carry — a badge, a trailing action, a submenu. Every part is a directive on your own element, and each one knows what to do on the icon rail, where only the row's icon is left.
