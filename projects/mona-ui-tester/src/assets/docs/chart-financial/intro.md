# Financial

**Selectors:** `mona-candlestick-series`, `mona-ohlc-series`

Candlestick and OHLC series draw the open, high, low and close of each period, such as a trading day, a week or a month. A candlestick shows the range between open and close as a body with wicks reaching to the high and low, and an OHLC bar shows the same four prices as a vertical line with a tick to the left for the open and to the right for the close. Each mark takes the rising, falling or neutral color by its direction. They go inside a `mona-chart` with an X and a Y axis, usually a time axis, and can share the chart with line, area and the other Cartesian series.
