# Funnel

**Selector:** `mona-funnel-series`

A funnel series draws the stages of a process, such as a sign-up flow or a sales pipeline, as connected bands whose width follows how many reached each stage. Each stage carries its conversion from the stage before, its conversion from the first stage, and how many dropped off, for labels, tooltips and screen readers. It goes inside a `mona-chart` on its own, with no axes.
