# Sparkline

**Selector:** `mona-sparkline`

`SparklineComponent` draws a series of values as a chart the size of a word, to sit on a line of text or in a table cell: a line, an area, columns or wins and losses. It has no axes, legend or tooltips; it shows a trend at a glance, next to the numbers that give it.

Screen readers read it as an image, with its name and a summary of its values: how many, the first, the last, the lowest and the highest.
