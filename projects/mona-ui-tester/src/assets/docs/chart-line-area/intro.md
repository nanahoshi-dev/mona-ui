# Line & Area

**Selectors:** `mona-line-series`, `mona-area-series`, `mona-range-area-series`

Line, area and range area series draw values that change along an X axis: over time, across categories or against a number. A line traces each value, an area fills down to the baseline or onto the series below it, and a range area fills the band between a low and a high value. They go inside a `mona-chart` with an X and a Y axis, and can share one chart with each other and with the other Cartesian series.
