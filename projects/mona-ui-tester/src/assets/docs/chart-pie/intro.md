# Pie & Donut

**Selectors:** `mona-pie-series`, `mona-donut-series`

Pie and donut series show how a whole splits into parts. Each row of data becomes a slice whose angle is its share of the total, and a donut leaves a hole in the middle for a total or a short summary. They go inside a `mona-chart` without axes, take one slice label per slice inside or outside the circle, and let the legend hide slices one by one.
