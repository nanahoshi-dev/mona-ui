# Bar & Waterfall

**Selectors:** `mona-bar-series`, `mona-range-bar-series`, `mona-waterfall-series`

Bar, range bar and waterfall series draw one rectangle per category. A bar compares an amount across categories, side by side with other series or stacked into a total, a range bar spans a low and a high value, and a waterfall walks a running total through increases, decreases, subtotals and a total. Bars and range bars can lie horizontally, and upright ones share a chart with the other Cartesian series. A waterfall has its chart to itself.
