# Scatter & Bubble

**Selectors:** `mona-scatter-series`, `mona-bubble-series`

Scatter and bubble series draw one marker per row at its X and Y value, to show how two numbers relate: whether they rise together, where they cluster and which rows stand apart. A bubble series adds a third number as the size of each marker. They go inside a `mona-chart` with a numeric or time X axis and a Y axis, and can share one chart with each other and with the other Cartesian series.
