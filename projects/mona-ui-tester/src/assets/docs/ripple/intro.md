# Ripple

A ripple is a wave that spreads from where a button is pressed, in the button's text color, and fades once it's let go. Ripples are off until you turn them on: `provideRipple()` gives one to Mona UI's buttons and the other components people press, from chips and menus to calendars, and the `monaRipple` directive adds one to elements of your own. Check boxes, radio buttons and switches draw a halo around the control instead.
